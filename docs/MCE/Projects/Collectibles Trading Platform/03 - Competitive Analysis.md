## Veriswap

**What it is:** an app where a middleman verifies and insures trades of sports and trading cards.

- ✅ Users add graded cards by certification number (PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC and others). Raw cards are allowed with condition details.
- ✅ Users propose trades, add cash to either side and counter-offer until both agree.
- ✅ Both sides ship to a Veriswap hub; cards are checked there and then forwarded.
- ✅ Fees are charged only on accepted trades. A newsletter mentions 4% of trade value per user, tied to Pokémon authentication; ⚠️ the date of that change is unclear.
- ✅ It is testing vaulting, so stored cards can be traded instantly.
- ✅ Sealed Pokémon product cannot be traded.
- ✅ The App Store shows a rating of about 4.7 from about 2,900 reviews.

**Strengths:** solves trust, the hardest part of trading. **Gaps:** not a full portfolio tracker or general marketplace; no sealed product.

## TCGplayer

**What it is:** a large US marketplace for trading card game singles (owned by eBay).

- ✅ Standard seller commission is about 10.75% (raised from 10.25% on 2026-02-10), with the per-item cap raised from $50 to $75.
- ✅ Payment processing is an extra 2.5% + $0.30 per order.
- ✅ Pro sellers pay a lower 9.25% commission, with a subscription.
- ✅ Its "Direct" programme (TCGplayer stores and ships the items) gets a new item-based fee structure from 2026-06-18.

**Strengths:** huge catalog and liquidity; widely used market prices. **Gaps:** no peer-to-peer trading; its collection tools are secondary to selling.

## eBay

**What it is:** a general marketplace with a large trading card category.

- ✅ **Authenticity Guarantee** for trading cards: eligible items ship to PSA for checking before going to the buyer. The US minimum price dropped from $250 to $200, rolling out from August 2026. It covers raw and graded singles.
- ✅ Launched in the UK in May 2026 for cards of £500 and up.
- ⚠️ The current final value fee for trading cards was **not confirmed**. Check eBay's seller fee page.

**Strengths:** largest audience, buyer protection, authentication for high-value cards. **Gaps:** generic experience; no trading; no portfolio.

## Collectr

**What it is:** a portfolio tracker for many trading card games.

- ✅ Tracks raw, graded and sealed items from a database of 200,000+ products; covers Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh! and others.
- ✅ Shows portfolio value, trends, and biggest gains and losses.
- ✅ Paid features (in-app purchase): unlimited scanning, advanced filters, data export.
- ⚠️ A competitor's site lists Collectr Pro at $7.99/month (June 2026). Not confirmed with Collectr.
- ✅ Links out to TCGplayer and eBay for buying missing cards.

**Strengths:** strong tracker that many collectors already use; covers many games. **Gaps:** sends buyers to other marketplaces; no peer-to-peer trading.

## Possible competitive advantages

1. **All three in one.** No competitor above does all three. ⚠️ This is only an advantage if users want them together. Being "a bit of everything" can also mean being best at nothing.
2. **Trade matching from real collections and wishlists.** Hard to copy without a tracker that people actually use.
3. **Pokémon focus.** Catalog, condition rules and community features built for one game.

## Threats

- **eBay owns TCGplayer**, which has prices, liquidity and money. If eBay adds trading or deeper tracking, it competes directly.
- **Collectr** already has users and the catalog; adding listings or trades is a natural next step for them.
- **Veriswap** already solved trust; adding portfolio features is easier than building a verification hub.
- Discord trading is free and established.
