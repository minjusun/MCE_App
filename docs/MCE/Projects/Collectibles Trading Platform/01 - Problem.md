# Problem

Back to [[Index]].

> [!warning] Status
> These problems come from how the market looks from the outside and from founder experience. ⚠️ **None have been confirmed with user interviews yet.** That is milestone 1 in [[Index]].

## 1. Too many apps

A collector who tracks, trades and sells today usually uses several tools:

| Job | Typical tool |
| --- | --- |
| Track value | Collectr, a spreadsheet, or nothing |
| Buy and sell singles | TCGplayer, eBay |
| Trade | Discord servers, Facebook groups, card shows, Veriswap |
| Talk to other collectors | Discord, Reddit, Instagram |

Each tool has its own copy of the collection. Listing a card for sale or offering it in a trade means typing it in again somewhere else. ⚠️ We assume this re-entry is painful enough to change behaviour.

## 2. Managing a portfolio is hard

- **Cost basis gets lost.** Pack openings, bulk buys and trades make it hard to know what a single card cost. Trades are especially hard because no cash changes hands.
- **Prices disagree.** Raw, graded (by grader and grade) and sealed items are priced from different sources and update at different rates. Collectors don't know which number to trust.
- **Data entry is slow.** Adding a large collection by hand is tedious; scanning helps but makes mistakes.

## 3. Trading is slow and risky

- **Trust:** in a Discord or Facebook trade, one person ships first and takes the risk. Fakes, resealed product and condition disputes are real concerns.
- **Valuing the trade:** both sides argue over which prices to use, and adding cash to even it out is awkward.
- **Finding a match:** finding someone who has what you want *and* wants what you have takes a lot of searching.
- **No record:** reputation lives in screenshots and server-specific "vouch" channels that don't carry between communities.

## 4. Selling costs a lot

Marketplace fees on cards are significant. On TCGplayer, sellers pay about 10.75% commission plus 2.5% + $0.30 payment processing (✅ per secondary sources, Oct 2026; see [[03 - Competitive Analysis]]). For lower-value cards, the fixed fees and shipping can wipe out the profit.

## Who feels it most (hypothesis)

⚠️ The collector who **trades and sells a few times a month** and has a collection large enough to track (hundreds of cards or more). Casual collectors may not need this much; high-volume sellers already have tooling.
