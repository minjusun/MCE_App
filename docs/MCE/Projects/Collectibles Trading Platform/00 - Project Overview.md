# Project Overview

Back to [[Index]].

## Vision

One place where a collector can see what their collection is worth, trade with people they trust, and buy and sell without switching apps.

## Mission

Make collecting easier to manage and trading safer, starting with Pokémon cards.

## Product summary

| Pillar | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Portfolio** | Track holdings (raw, graded, sealed), market value, cost basis and performance over time |
| **Trades** | Propose, counter and complete card-for-card trades, with optional cash to balance either side |
| **Marketplace** | List, discover, buy and sell |

All three run on one shared item catalog and one user profile, so a card in your portfolio can be listed for sale or offered in a trade with a tap. See [[02 - Solution]].

## Target audience

**Primary (launch):** Pokémon collectors in the US who already track value and trade or sell now and then: people who use spreadsheets or Collectr, trade in Discord servers, and sell on TCGplayer or eBay.

**Secondary (later):** small sellers and local card shops; other trading card games and sports cards.

⚠️ We have not yet measured the size of this audience or how many of them would switch. See [[08 - Risks and Assumptions]].

## Core value proposition

> **Your collection, its value, and every way to trade it, in one app.**

- **Portfolio first:** useful on day one even if none of your friends are on the app.
- **Trades that come from your collection:** your holdings and wishlist are already in the app, so finding a trade match takes less work.
- **Safer trades:** both sides agree terms in the app, with clear records and (later) verification. See [[03 - Competitive Analysis]] for how Veriswap does this today.
