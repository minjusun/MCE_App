# Go To Market

Back to [[Index]].

> [!warning] Status
> Plan only. ⚠️ No channel below has been tested. Measure results for each before spending money on it.

## Launch audience

Pokémon collectors in the US who:
- already track their collection (spreadsheet or Collectr), and
- trade or sell at least occasionally (Discord, Facebook groups, TCGplayer, eBay, card shows).

Start with the few hundred most active people we can reach directly, not the whole market.

## Community-driven acquisition

- **Build in public:** share progress, screenshots and the roadmap in collector communities. Ask for feedback, not sign-ups.
- **Shareable binders:** each user's public binder has a link that looks good when posted in Discord or on social media. Every share is a small advert.
- **Content collectors already want:** price movers, set value summaries, "cards under market price". Generated from our own price data.
- **Card shows and local shops:** show the app in person; a QR code that leads straight to a public binder.

## Discord communities

- Join existing Pokémon trading servers as a member first. Don't spam; follow each server's rules on self-promotion.
- Offer server owners something useful: e.g. a bot that shows card prices or links to binders. ⚠️ Only if it doesn't break Discord's or the server's rules.
- Run our own server for early testers: feedback, bug reports, feature voting.
- ⚠️ Assumption: server owners will welcome a tool that makes trading safer. They may see us as a competitor instead.

## Seller onboarding (before Phase 2)

A marketplace with no listings loses buyers on the first visit. So:
- Recruit 10–20 sellers before the marketplace opens (small sellers, local card shops, active Discord traders).
- Offer reduced or zero fees for an introductory period (⚠️ length and terms to be decided).
- Import their inventory for them, or make import from a CSV or their portfolio easy.
- Start with one narrow category (e.g. modern Pokémon singles) so listings feel complete in that area.

## Early adopter strategy

1. **Interviews (now):** 15–20 conversations with collectors and traders. Validate [[01 - Problem]].
2. **Closed beta (Phase 1):** invite-only from interviews and Discord. Weekly feedback.
3. **Founding member perks:** e.g. free premium for a period, a founder badge on the binder, reduced fees later. ⚠️ Must not create costs we can't afford.
4. **Referrals:** invite a friend to unlock something useful (e.g. more price alerts). Avoid cash rewards until fraud controls exist.

## What to measure

| Stage | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition | Sign-ups per channel |
| Activation | % who add 10+ items in the first session |
| Retention | % still active after 4 weeks |
| Marketplace (Phase 2) | Active listings; % of listings sold within 30 days |
| Trading (Phase 3) | Trades proposed vs completed; disputes per 100 trades |
