# Pitch Deck

Back to [[Index]]. Internal draft; one heading = one slide. ⚠️ marks claims we still need to validate.

---

## 1. Title

- **BINDER** (working title): collect, trade, sell.
- Track your Pokémon collection, trade it safely, and buy and sell it in one app.

---

## 2. Problem

- Collectors use several apps: one to track, others to sell, Discord to trade.
- Cost basis and true value are hard to keep track of, especially after trades.
- Trades are risky: someone ships first and hopes.
- Details: [[01 - Problem]]. ⚠️ To be confirmed in interviews.

---

## 3. Solution

- **Portfolio:** value, cost basis and performance for raw, graded and sealed items.
- **Marketplace:** list straight from your portfolio with a suggested price.
- **Trades:** build trades from both binders, add cash, counter, complete.
- Details: [[02 - Solution]].

---

## 4. Why now

- Existing tools each cover one job: Collectr tracks, TCGplayer and eBay sell, Veriswap trades.
- Big platforms are investing in trust for cards (e.g. eBay lowered its authentication threshold to $200 in 2026).
- ⚠️ We still need our own evidence of demand for an all-in-one app.

---

## 5. Product

- Mobile app (iOS + Android), fintech-style dashboard.
- Screens: Home, Portfolio, Shop, Trades, Wishlist, Binder, Messages.
- Status: wireframe done; app scaffolded; no live product yet.

---

## 6. Market

- Launch: US Pokémon collectors who track and trade.
- Later: other trading card games, sports cards, other collectibles.
- ⚠️ Market size not yet estimated from reliable sources. **No numbers until they are sourced.**

---

## 7. Competition

| | Track | Trade | Sell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collectr | ✅ | ❌ | links out |
| Veriswap | limited | ✅ | ❌ |
| TCGplayer / eBay | limited | ❌ | ✅ |
| **Us** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

- Our edge: trades and listings come from a real, tracked collection.
- Details: [[03 - Competitive Analysis]].

---

## 8. Business model

- Marketplace fee on sales (below TCGplayer's ~10.75% + processing). ⚠️ Rate undecided.
- Fees on protected trades, optional verification.
- Premium analytics subscription.
- Details: [[04 - Business Model]].

---

## 9. Go-to-market and roadmap

- Phase 1 tracker → 2 marketplace → 3 trading → 4 analytics + verification.
- Community first: Discord, shareable binders, card shows.
- Recruit sellers before the marketplace opens.
- Details: [[05 - MVP Roadmap]], [[06 - Go To Market]].

---
